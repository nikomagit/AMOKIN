# LATAM TV poster sources

The 2:3 poster cards in `posters/` were generated for AMOKIN from public channel
logos indexed by the [IPTV-org logos API](https://iptv-org.github.io/api/logos.json).
The channel-to-logo mapping and the few Wikimedia/Fubo fallbacks are recorded in
`scripts/build-latam-tv-posters.py`. Brand names and logos remain the property of
their respective owners.
